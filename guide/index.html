04 · the flex

Brag
cards ✦

Instagram Story images (1080 × 1920), made from your own shelf and drawn right on your phone. Six of them, four colours. These are the real cards, drawn live from Nitya's pile in the tour above: finish a book or save a line there and watch them change. Tap one to open the actual card maker.

Where: Mine → "Make a brag card ✦". Or "Brag about it ✦" on any finished book, which opens that book's card directly.
Posting it: tap "Share", pick Instagram, then Stories. No Share button on your phone? "Save image", then add it from your photos.
Not earned yet? The card says how to unlock it: "Tap 'I read today' to start a streak."
Private books never appear on a card. Nothing is uploaded: the card is made on your phone and goes only where you send it.
05 · for @pagesofpanvel

The circle card

The whole circle's month on one card: books finished, readers, genres, languages, the longest streak and the most-read book. No names on it: the circle's numbers are the story. Find it on the Ranks tab, "Make the circle card ✦".

Sample numbers. The real card counts the real circle.

07 · the small print, but friendly

Who sees what

The circle sees

  • Your name, as it is on your Google account
  • Your public books, their tags, and when you finish them
  • The lines you save
  • Which of your books you'd lend
  • Your streak and places on the standings
  • Your agenda posts

Only you see

  • Your private books. That's the whole list.
  • Your email: the circle only ever sees your name.

Brag cards are made on your phone and nothing is uploaded; you choose what to post. To find covers, the titles and authors of public books are looked up on Open Library. Private books never are.
