about us.

@pagesofpanvel is a reading community that aims to bring together beautiful people such as yourself who share a common passion for books!

In this fast paced world, we often forget to take a couple of minutes to ourselves and just read.

How it works

Silent reading

We bring our own books, sit together in a nice spot and read in peace.

A book circle at 10

Then we gather and share what we've been reading. No compulsion: listen, or join in.

Open to everyone

It is free and open to all!

Every Sunday

8:30–10am, join anytime. In New Panvel; we'll share the location via email.

More than reading

At the heart of this is a trust-based book exchange system. Readers bring along books they’re willing to lend or pass on, creating an informal open library.

“There’s no tracking system. You take a book, you return it when you can, pass it on, or even donate a book. But the idea is to donate what you yourself would read.”

A member

What truly sets Pages of Panvel apart is how it moves beyond just reading. Once the silent session ends, the space naturally shifts into community-led interactions — from relaxed sharing circles to activities like Secret Santa book exchanges — where conversations around what people are reading unfold with ease.
